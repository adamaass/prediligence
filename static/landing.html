How it works

Five agents. One memo. Two minutes.

Each stage reads from and writes to the fund's fact memory, so stale data and changes are detected on the way, not after.

01

Planner

Turns a startup name into six targeted searches: team, funding, product, competitors, news, X.

02

Search

Runs them in parallel on the live web. Each page comes back with its publish date and fetch date.

03

Extract

Pulls structured facts out of the pages. A fact without a URL is rejected.

04

Verify

Confronts new facts with each other and with memory: confirmed, new, contradicted or stale.

05

Memo

Writes the pre-call memo from verified facts only, plus five questions to ask the founder.
