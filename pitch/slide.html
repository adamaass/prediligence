Data-Centric Agents · Paris

The AI analyst that turns a startup name into a sourced pre-call memo in 2 minutes, and flags what the databases got wrong.

Who it's for
  • Analysts and partners at pre-seed and seed funds, who screen hundreds of startups a month.
  • Each first look takes 2 to 4 hours of research on public data that is scattered, stale and contradictory.
  • Incumbents serve PE, not seed: DiligenceSquared charges ~$50k per project.
Why now
  • Live web search built for agents is now cheap and fast (Keenable).
  • LLMs can finally extract and cross-check facts, not just summarise.
  • The market pays: Rogo passed $50M ARR, and Accel co-led Bridgetown Research's $19M Series A. Nobody serves early stage.
First users
  • The VCs in this room: free pilot from tomorrow, on their real deal flow.
  • Paris seed funds, scout programs and angel networks, through warm intros.
  • A weekly public post: "what the databases got wrong this week". Then €200–500 per seat per month.
How it works · data at the core
Keenable live web + X→ Planner→ 4 research agents→ Fact extractor→ Verifier→ Memo + 5 call questions
Aiven PostgreSQL is the memory. Every fact is stored with its source, date and confidence. No source, no fact. The primary source beats stale databases, and every re-check shows exactly what changed.
